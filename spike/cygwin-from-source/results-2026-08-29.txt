== question 2: does the reservation keep the red zone whole?

    verdict                    UNANSWERED. The probe is not sensitive to the
                               frame it was built to watch, and the
                               differential is what proves it.

    single word at -8, stock installed dll
        deliveries             813
        covered rounds         812
        broken                 0
    control arm, a call into the same window
        rounds                 2000
        broken                 2000       first clobber 0x1004010ca
        reads                  the control detects a clobber at -8 reliably,
                               so the instrument is not simply blind

    512-byte window, stock installed dll
        deliveries             872
        covered rounds         871
        nearest write          296
        furthest write         512 (the window edge)
    512-byte window, patched dll
        deliveries             1020
        covered rounds         1020
        nearest write          264
        furthest write         512

    A frame shifted down by exactly 128 bytes must move the nearest observed
    write down by 128. It moved by -32, in the wrong direction, which is the
    size of ordinary run-to-run variation here. So what this probe sees at
    264 to 512 is not sigdelayed's frame, and its silence between 8 and 264
    is not evidence that the red zone survived.

== what this does not say

    It does not say Cygwin 3.6.10 preserves the red zone. That reading is
    available from the numbers above and it is not supported by them.

    It does not contradict spike 3, which measured nearest:8 on Cygwin 3.0.7
    in the RHEL emulation. Two probes on two Cygwin versions disagree and the
    reason is unestablished: it may be the version, or it may be that one of
    them watches a stack the delivery does not use.

    It does not license retiring -mno-red-zone. DR-0006 stands exactly as
    written.
