what this rules out

    A third arm conditioned on PIC cannot work. The failing object and
    a static-libc object agree on both flags, so any condition written
    over them puts both in the same arm, which is where they already
    are. glibc does not know at compile time whether the program its
    object will join is linked statically or dynamically, and nothing
    in the flags can be made to say so.
