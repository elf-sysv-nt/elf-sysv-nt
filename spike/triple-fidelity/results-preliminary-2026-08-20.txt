== why the control was accepted

    Probing one 2021 config.sub, which validates the os field where the
    2013 and 2019 vintages here do not.

    elfsysvnt    rc=0  x86_64-pc-elfsysvnt
    elfsysv      rc=0  x86_64-pc-elfsysv
    elfnt        rc=0  x86_64-pc-elfnt
    sysvnt       rc=0  x86_64-pc-sysvnt
    nt           rc=1  Invalid configuration `x86_64-pc-nt': OS `nt' not recognized
    notarealos   rc=1  Invalid configuration `x86_64-pc-notarealos': OS `notarealos' not recognized
    zzz          rc=1  Invalid configuration `x86_64-pc-zzz': OS `zzz' not recognized

    The accepted os list carries elf* for bare-metal ELF targets, so
    elfsysvnt matches it. Acceptance is an accident of a glob, not a
    grant, and gcc config.gcc reads x86_64-*-elf* as bare metal. An
    os field starting with elf is therefore worse than a rejected one.

    	     | bosx* | nextstep* | cxux* | aout* | elf* | oabi* \
