== config.sub verdicts, one row per file probed

                   probed  accepted rewritten  rejected      miss
    masquerade       1193      1173         1        19      1.7%
    candidate        1193      1173         1        19      1.7%
    control          1193      1173         1        19      1.7%

    Accepted means the triple came back byte for byte. Rewritten means
    config.sub canonicalized it into something else, which is a silent
    miss and the shape that matters. The control is expected to be
    rejected outright; a row that is not tells you the os field is
    cheaper than assumed.

    literal vendor in a source      19   0.7% of seen
    both                             7
    affected either way             32   1.1% of seen

    The literal count is an upper bound. The pattern matches a comment
    and a live host test alike, so a nonzero figure wants reading
    before it is quoted.

    The second row is the one that prices the decision. A config.sub
    that refuses the candidate and refuses the masquerade too is
    refusing something else about the triple, usually the cpu field,
    and the vendor should not be charged for it.
