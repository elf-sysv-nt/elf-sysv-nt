== reading

    The wide scope found no signal the narrow scope missed. It found
    noise, and enough of it to report 12.5% of packages affected,
    which is over the share at which DR-0001 says the triple gets
    reconsidered. A decision reopened on that would have been reopened
    on false positives.

    count-vendor-misses.sh now excludes those shapes, and t/lit-noise
    carries one package per shape so a dropped rule shows up as a
    count of two rather than one.

    One hit survives the new rules and should: a hand-written .sh naming
    a triple. Excluding shell outright would be excluding the one place a
    genuine hand-rolled host test could hide, so the literal figure stays
    an upper bound, and its remaining false positives are CI scripts.
    A nonzero literal count still wants reading before it is quoted.

    Forty packages off the head of a sorted list prices the runtime and
    characterises the noise. It says nothing about how many el8
    packages hand-roll a host test.
